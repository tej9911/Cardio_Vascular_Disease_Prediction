Key Insights & Statistical Significance

  • Tree Models vs Linear Models: Gradient Boosting and Random Forest algorithms successfully modeled complex, non-linear patient interactions (e.g. combination of age and high blood pressure).
  • Clinical Drivers: Statistical analysis identified Diabetes, Tobacco Smoking, and Family History as the most significant clinical risk factors ($p < 0.001$).
  • Recall vs Precision: Given the high cost of false negatives in medical diagnostics, models were optimized to maintain high sensitivity (recall) to capture early-stage risk profiles.
